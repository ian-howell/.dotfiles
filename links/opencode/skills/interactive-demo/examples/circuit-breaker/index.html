Notes

An invented example for the interactive-demo skill: a consecutive-failure circuit breaker with a threshold of 3 and a 30-second cooldown.

  • The Go excerpts come from a small illustrative breaker.go written for this demo, not from a real library.
  • Half-open allows exactly one trial call. Real breakers often allow several, and track failure rates rather than consecutive failures.
  • Time only moves when the story or the playground advances it.
